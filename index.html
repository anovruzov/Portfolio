Research

Can an agentic web remember without centralizing memory?

That question drives most of what I’m building. My current work explores lineage, independent support, self-healing reconstruction, and the failure modes that appear when memory is distributed across agents.

The working paper is The Mycelial Web: Self-Healing Collective Memory for the Agentic Internet.
