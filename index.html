Research thesis

Can an Agentic Web preserve useful knowledge without centralizing memory?

That is the question behind most of my current work. I’m exploring how knowledge can persist across independent agents through lineage, redundancy, local memory, and reconstruction rather than a single shared store.

Current paper: The Mycelial Web: Self-Healing Collective Memory for the Agentic Internet.
